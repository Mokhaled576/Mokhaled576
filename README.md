# Mostafa Khaled

### Graduate Researcher | Cardiovascular Pharmacology & Computational Biology

I am a graduate researcher at the University of Alberta working at the intersection of cardiovascular pharmacology, molecular biology, and computational transcriptomics.

My research focuses on understanding molecular and cellular mechanisms involved in cardiovascular disease, aging, and neurodegenerative disorders. I am particularly interested in combining experimental pharmacology with single-cell and spatial transcriptomic approaches to investigate disease mechanisms at cellular resolution.

## Research Interests

- Cardiovascular pharmacology
- Cytochrome P450 (CYP) enzymes and arachidonic acid metabolism
- Eicosanoid signaling
- Cardiovascular remodeling and heart failure
- Estrogen-associated signaling
- Single-cell and single-nucleus RNA sequencing
- Spatial transcriptomics
- Computational biology and bioinformatics
- Reproducible transcriptomic analysis in R

## Selected Computational Research

### Female Ventricular Cardiomyocyte Aging
Age-associated transcriptional remodeling of ventricular cardiomyocytes in the non-failing human female heart, with emphasis on CYP-mediated metabolism, estrogen-associated signaling, and lipid/eicosanoid pathways.

[View repository](https://github.com/Mokhaled576/female-LV-VCM-aging)

### CYP–Arachidonic Acid Axis in Cardiac Remodeling
Cellular and spatial investigation of the cytochrome P450–arachidonic acid pathway during pressure-overload cardiac remodeling.

[View repository](https://github.com/Mokhaled576/CYP-AA-TAC-Remodeling)

### APP23 Hippocampal snRNA-seq
Single-nucleus transcriptomic analysis investigating why specific hippocampal neuronal populations undergo extensive transcriptional remodeling in APP23 mice while others remain relatively preserved.

[View repository](https://github.com/Mokhaled576/APP23-hippocampal-snRNAseq)
